Model and environment

Randomize uses demo seeds screened for fixed-height flight, precise stopping, a final approach without loops, and a total path at most 1.35 times the start-to-goal distance. Other obstacle counts are screened with live inference before display. Manually entered seeds and edited scenes are unscreened. These selected examples are separate from the paper's benchmarks.

The publication weights run locally with web terminal tuning in ONNX Runtime Web. Each step renders a new 160 x 96 axial-depth image (90 x 60 degree field of view, 20 m range) and predicts 24 trajectory endpoints and costs. This level-flight demo projects candidates to the chosen height and ranks geometrically feasible candidates by predicted cost. Obstacle footprints are checked so the vehicle cannot fly over them.

A separate web execution controller checks a smooth quintic stop near the goal and follows it to rest when its path is clear. This controller uses the generated scene geometry; it is not part of the paper's learned planner. Its settings and seed selection are recorded in run exports. Flights are computed afresh, without recorded trajectories.

Tracking follows the analytic trajectory reference at 10 Hz, without rigid-body dynamics, sensor noise or transport delay. Collision uses a 0.22 m vehicle radius. Arrival requires 3D error at most 0.1 m and speed at most 0.2m/s for 0.5 s. Runs stop on collision, scene exit or a 40 s timeout. These interactive scenes are separate from the paper's physical simulation and evaluation results.
