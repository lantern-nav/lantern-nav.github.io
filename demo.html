LANTERN model · Custom scene · Ideal trajectory tracking

Model and environment

Randomize uses success-screened demo seeds. Other obstacle counts are screened with live inference before display. These selected examples are separate from the paper's benchmarks. Manually entered seeds and edited scenes are unscreened.

The publication weights run locally with web terminal tuning in ONNX Runtime Web. Terminal queries retain the 5 m / 2 m activation range and use zero control residual. Each step renders a new 160 x 96 axial-depth image (90 x 60 degree field of view, 20 m range) and predicts 24 trajectory endpoints and costs. The minimum predicted cost selects the executable quintic curve. The scene is editable; no recorded flight is used.

Tracking follows the analytic trajectory reference at 10 Hz, without rigid-body dynamics, sensor noise or transport delay. Collision uses a 0.22 m vehicle radius. Arrival requires 3D error at most 0.1 m and speed at most 0.2m/s for 0.5 s. Runs stop on collision, scene exit or a 40 s timeout. These interactive scenes are separate from the paper's physical simulation and evaluation results.
